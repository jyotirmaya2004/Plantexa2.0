Essential Quick Crop Care Tips

Maintain proper micro-environmental factors to boost crop immunity and prevent re-infection.

Sunlight & Light Exposure

Ensure 6–8 hours of sunlight daily. Natural UV light promotes photosynthesis and suppresses surface fungal spore germination.

Smart Irrigation & Soil Moisture

Irrigate directly at the root zone early morning. Avoid overhead leaf spraying to keep foliage dry and reduce humidity risks.

Canopy Air Flow & Spacing

Prune dense lower foliage and maintain optimal row spacing to ensure active air circulation and lower canopy moisture accumulation.

About Plantexa AI

Plantexa AI is an intelligent end-to-end plant leaf disease detection platform designed to help farmers, gardeners, and agronomists diagnose crop diseases early and improve yield protection.

Architecture Highlights:

  • Two-Stage ML Pipeline: Stage 1 validates whether the image is a plant leaf; Stage 2 classifies 38 disease categories.
  • Explainable AI (Grad-CAM): Generates visual attention heatmaps so users can trust and understand the model's focus.
  • Agriculture AI Assistant: NVIDIA LLM API integration customized specifically for agronomy.
  • Flask Microservice Architecture: Fast, lightweight HTTP REST APIs with standard JSON contracts.
